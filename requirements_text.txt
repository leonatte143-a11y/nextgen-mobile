 KAIRO: Official Cancellation Policy

1. The "Golden Window" (Full Refund)

     Rule: Users can cancel any booking up to 2 hours before the scheduled service time for
         a 100% refund to their KAIRO Wallet or original payment method.

     Logic: This gives the partner enough time to find another booking.

2. The "Short-Notice" Penalty

     Rule: Cancellations made within 2 hours of the scheduled time will incur a
         Cancellation Fee of 50.

     Logic: This fee is credited to the Service Partner (Phani) to compensate for their travel
         time or missed opportunity.

3. "Partner at Doorstep" Policy

     Rule: If the partner has already arrived at the location (verified via GPS), and the user
         cancels, a Visiting Fee of 100 is mandatory.

     Exception: If the partner is more than 20 minutes late beyond the scheduled time, the
         user can cancel for free.
